About the Hub

Learn. Prepare. Go Abroad.

Kigali Language Training Hub is a language school dedicated to helping candidates master French and reach the scores they need for their next opportunity.

From our centre on 1st Floor, Centenary House, Kigali City, we support learners preparing for French language tests used for Canadian and Québec immigration, French citizenship applications, and internationally recognised DELF and DALF diplomas.
